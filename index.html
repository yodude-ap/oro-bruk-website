The Visionary Behind the Weave

Metho's Moma

Founder, Oro Bruk Federation

Oro Bruk was born as a direct response to a changing climate and a deep love for Monpa heritage. Observing that traditional, heavy wool garments were increasingly being left in suitcases during Itanagar's scorching summers, Metho's Moma refused to let her culture fade into history.

She pioneered a revolution in indigenous fashion by adapting ancient Monpa weaves into breathable, weather-conscious fabrics like Eri silk, crepe, and light cottons—making the attire comfortable and wearable year-round.

With profound respect for tradition, she spends months researching to ensure sacred symbols, like the revered elephant motif, are placed with utmost cultural reverence. Today, her vision bridges the gap between heritage and modern youth, upskilling local women on traditional blackstrap looms and ensuring the Monpa identity thrives for generations to come.

Administrative Backbone

Miss Tenzin Metoh

Secretary, Oro Bruk Federation

Serving as the operational cornerstone of the Oro Bruk Federation, Miss Tenzin Metoh ensures that our expansive vision translates into daily, impactful reality across the region.

Her leadership bridges the gap between traditional artisans and modern administrative efficiency. By managing community outreach and organizing cooperative frameworks, she provides local women and farmers with the structured support they need to thrive in a growing economy.

Beyond logistics, she is a nurturing presence within the federation, guiding our educational and sports initiatives with maternal care and a fierce dedication to the empowerment of the next generation.

From Loom to Legacy

Founded in 2020 by visionaries Metho's Moma and Genden Sangmo, Oro Bruk began as an apparel brand dedicated to bridging traditional North-East Indian attire with contemporary design. Our early years were defined by intensive R&D alongside master weavers in the Mon region to pioneer the first-ever coats in historic Monpa and nomadic Brokpa patterns.

Recognizing that true empowerment requires a holistic approach, Oro Bruk transformed into a comprehensive NGO in December 2024. Today, operating across Itanagar and Kalaktang, the Oro Bruk Federation stands as a unified force uplifting our people through textiles, agriculture, and youth development.

Our Foundation

The Core Pillars

Our multi-faceted approach ensures that economic growth, cultural preservation, and environmental stewardship go hand in hand.

Handloom

Empowering indigenous artisans by reviving traditional blackstrap loom techniques, weaving ancient motifs into sustainable, world-class garments.

Education

Fostering holistic community growth by providing access to foundational learning, preserving tribal dialects, and equipping our youth with modern skills.

Environment

Championing eco-conscious practices, from promoting natural, plant-based dyes to leading community conservation drives in the Himalayan foothills.

Sports

Nurturing rural talent and promoting physical well-being by organizing regional tournaments that build leadership, teamwork, and lifelong resilience.

Agriculture

Strengthening rural economies through strategic farmer cooperatives. We champion sustainable farming practices and connect local growers to wider markets.

Livestock & Cattle Feed

Enhancing the livelihoods of regional herders by supporting nomadic pastoral traditions, providing nutrient-rich feed, and ensuring the health of cattle and yaks.

National Recognition

A Coat Woven with Love, Worn with Honour

In September 2025, our mission received a profound national spotlight. The Hon'ble Union Minister of Civil Aviation, Shri Ram Mohan Naidu Kinjarapu, adorned the hand-woven Oro Bruk Federation Monpa Coat.

It wasn't just fabric he wore—it was the result of a month of rigorous labor, dedication, and patience by our artisans. Moments like these validate our unwavering commitment: what looks effortlessly elegant is, in truth, the quiet strength and endless dedication of our indigenous weavers.
